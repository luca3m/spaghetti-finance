Moving abroad brings a host of logistical challenges, and one of the most critical is maintaining connectivity through your mobile phone. Whether it's staying in touch with loved ones or ensuring access to essential services like online banking, having an active phone number is essential. However, traditional carrier plans can be costly and often include unnecessary features.

Here are some key considerations and cost-effective solutions to keep your US mobile phone active and optimised for your needs while living abroad:

  • Staying Connected with Friends and Family: your phone number serves as a vital link to your social network back home. Keeping it active ensures that friends and family can reach you easily, regardless of your location.

  • Ensuring Security with 2-Factor Authentication: many online services, particularly those related to finance and security, rely on 2-factor authentication (2FA) for added security. Having access to your US mobile number ensures seamless verification processes, maintaining the integrity of your accounts.

  • Exploring Cost-Effective Alternatives: while maintaining your carrier plan is an option, it's often expensive and includes features you may not need, such as excessive data or unlimited calls. Exploring alternative providers can offer significant cost savings without sacrificing connectivity.

Options for US Expats

  • US Mobile: US Mobile offers a compelling solution for expats with its affordable plans tailored to international needs. With plans starting at just $6 per month, you can maintain an active US mobile number without breaking the bank. These plans provide essential services without unnecessary frills, making them ideal for expats seeking cost-effective connectivity.

  • Google Voice: another option is Google Voice, which offers a free solution for keeping your US number active abroad. While there's a one-time $20 fee to port your number, the ongoing service is free. However, it's worth noting that Google Voice operates differently from traditional SIM-based services, which may occasionally present challenges with certain applications like iMessage and 2FA.
